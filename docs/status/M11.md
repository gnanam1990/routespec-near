## Red/green account (honest)

Tests were written against the unchanged product first; the red runs were real:

- **T-040** (run against the old `bundle.ts`): 6 of 9 failed. The export was written to
  `work/NUL`, `work/con.tar`, `work/COM1.json`, `bundle.tar.`, `bundle.tar ` and a name with
  a right-to-left override; a junction at the target and a dangling junction were not refused
  as links; every hostile archive case reported an escape or extracted `nul`, `con.json`,
  `aux/…`, `com1.txt`, `manifest.json.`; extraction followed a junction into another directory;
  `export verify` on a UNC bundle path answered `RS_INTERNAL`; imported names `trace.json.` and
  `trace.json ` were stored unchanged. One stray artifact of the red run (`routespec-t040.tar`
  written for the drive-relative path `C:routespec-t040.tar`) was removed before committing.
- **T-042** (old control service): 8 of 11 failed: an endless chunked upload was read for 6 s,
  slow clients survived 15 s, 232 idle connections were held open, 3,000 junk proxy requests
  wrote 3,000 audit rows, no metrics endpoint. Later, the registry audit led to a new case:
  2,000 nested YAML flow brackets made scenario validation answer `RS_INTERNAL` (500); fixed by
  the pre-scan and `yaml` 2.8.4.
- **T-011** (entry point with hand-off, before the secret fixes): 4 of 8 failed. The configured
  secret and the control token were in `journal.db-wal` (project names), the configured secret
  was in `logs/control.jsonl` (the raw proxy path of a denied request), and both were in the
  console DOM of five pages; an idempotency key and a cursor with the secret were accepted or
  answered without refusal; the quote field and the logical-job header were not refused before
  scenario consumption.
- **T-036** passed on its first run: the loopback-only binding existed already. Its mutations
  (below) show that the test would catch a regression.
- First green runs exposed test-side mistakes, fixed in the tests: a schema-invalid sample trace,
  a scenario without a delay step, a missing run-operation header on a direct proxy call, and
  PowerShell JSON that cannot carry control characters in command lines (now base64 per line).
- Under full-suite load one T-040 run failed with `RS_EXPORT_WRITE_FAILED` on the confirmed
  overwrite (a transient Windows sharing violation on the freshly written file). The rename is
  now retried for at most about 1 s on `EPERM`/`EACCES`/`EBUSY`; three repeated runs and the full
  suite passed afterwards.

## Security and privacy review (this team; not the independent review)

- No secret value is printed, logged, or committed. Canaries are random per run and exist only
  in test memory, the child's memory, and (by design) the browser's in-memory token.
- The one-time console URL is no longer written to a captured stderr.
- Local listeners: four listener kinds verified from the OS; all loopback.
- Effect boundary: subject reports cannot claim the boundary source; no HTTP route writes
  boundary observations; containment is `DECLARED_HOOKS_ONLY` here and the gap is visible in
  `/metrics`.
- Data protection: owner-only modes are requested for the data and log directories; on Windows
  the profile ACLs apply instead (mode bits are not enforced).

## Known limits

- The configured-secret check matches exact substrings. An encoded or split copy of a secret in
  a request is not recognized; T-011 proves the injected forms only.
- Retention purge does not clear MAPPED/REPLAY_VERIFIED/REVIEWED/PROMOTED imports (their
  derived records are immutable) and does not purge projects physically; logical deletion
  makes their quarantine content due at once. Physical media erasure is not claimed.
- The journal write guard checks `run()` parameters inside journal transactions; `exec()`
  statements with inline literals are not checked (the product uses them only for DDL and
  pragmas).
- Not run on Linux or macOS; the `lsof` and `/proc` branches of `processes.ts` are untested.
- No process-tree sandbox; complete-effect coverage is never reported.
